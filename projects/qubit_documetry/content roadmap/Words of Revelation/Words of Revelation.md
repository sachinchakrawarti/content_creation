# REVELATION

## Quran Verse Shorts Series

**Series Name:** REVELATION
**Format:** YouTube Shorts
**Category:** Quran / Islamic Content
**Channel:** Qubit Documentary

---

## Series Concept

**REVELATION** is a cinematic short-form series presenting selected verses from the Quran through beautiful visuals, authentic Arabic recitation, accurate translations, and concise reflections.

Each episode focuses on **one Quranic verse or a closely connected set of verses**, allowing the audience to pause, listen, understand, and reflect on the message.

The goal is not simply to display a verse, but to create a **quiet moment of reflection** around its meaning.

---

## Episode Format

Each short should generally follow this structure:

### 1. Opening — 0–3 sec

A cinematic visual and subtle hook that establishes the mood.

### 2. Revelation — 3–20 sec

Display the Quranic Arabic text while the verse is recited.

### 3. Translation — 20–35 sec

Present an accurate English and/or Hindi translation.

### 4. Reflection — 35–50 sec

A short, thoughtful explanation of the verse's central message.

### 5. Closing — 50–60 sec

Display:

**Surah Name — Ayah Number**

followed by subtle **REVELATION** branding.

---

## Visual Direction

* Cinematic and atmospheric
* Minimal and respectful
* Slow camera movement
* Natural landscapes and abstract light
* Deep shadows and subtle highlights
* Elegant Arabic typography
* Clean subtitles
* No unnecessary visual distractions
* Avoid depicting prophets or sacred figures
* Maintain a reverent tone throughout

---

## Audio Direction

### Quran Recitation

Use an authentic Quran recitation and clearly identify the reciter/source where required.

### Background Music

Prefer silence, natural ambience, or extremely subtle atmospheric sound where appropriate.

The Quranic recitation should always remain the primary audio element.

---

## Typography

### Arabic

Use a highly readable Quranic Arabic font with correct shaping and diacritics.

### Translation

Clean modern typography with strong readability on mobile screens.

### Series Title

**REVELATION**

Keep the branding subtle so that it never competes with the Quranic text.

---

```text
REVELATION-0001/

├── research.md
├── references.md
└── storyboard.md
```

---

## Research Standards

Every episode must verify:

* Surah name
* Surah number
* Ayah number
* Exact Arabic text
* Translation source
* Tafsir/context where applicable
* Recitation source
* Visual references

Do not paraphrase the Arabic verse as though it were the Quranic text.

Translations and interpretations should be clearly distinguished from the original Arabic.

---

## Content Philosophy

**One Ayah.
One Moment.
One Reflection.**

The series should encourage viewers to stop scrolling for a moment and engage with the meaning of the Quran.
