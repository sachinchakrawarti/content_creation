# Transition Direction

* No abrupt cuts unless required for rhythm.
* Prefer slow dissolves and cinematic fades.
* Keep transitions subtle.
* Avoid flashy effects.
* Allow the verse to remain the visual focus.

---

# Text Safety

* Verify Arabic text character-by-character against a reliable Quran source.
* Do not alter Quranic wording for visual purposes.
* Preserve diacritics where applicable.
* Verify Surah and Ayah numbers.
* Clearly distinguish Quranic text from translation and commentary.
* Avoid placing important Arabic text over visually busy areas.

---

# Visual Restrictions

Avoid:

* Depictions of Allah
* Depictions of prophets
* Depictions of angels as literal representations
* Sensational or disrespectful imagery
* Excessive visual effects
* Horror-style treatment
* Unnecessary human dramatization of sacred events
