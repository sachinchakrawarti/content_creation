# Hook (0–5 seconds)

Have you ever wondered why space is completely dark, even though the Sun is shining with incredible brightness?

---

# Main Script (5–45 seconds)

The Sun emits enough light to illuminate our entire Solar System. So why doesn't space glow?

The answer is surprisingly simple.

Light only becomes visible when it reflects off an object and reaches your eyes. On Earth, sunlight bounces off air molecules, clouds, buildings, and the ground, making the sky appear bright.

But space is almost a perfect vacuum. There are very few particles to scatter sunlight, so most of the light travels through empty space without reflecting toward you.

That's why, unless you're looking directly at the Sun or a lit object like Earth or the Moon, space appears pitch black.

---

# Ending (45–60 seconds)

So, space isn't dark because there's no light—it's dark because there's almost nothing there to reflect it.
