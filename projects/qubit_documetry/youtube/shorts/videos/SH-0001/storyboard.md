# Video Objective

Explain why outer space appears black even though the Sun is extremely bright, using simple visuals and an engaging narrative suitable for a general audience.

---

# Storyboard Timeline

| Time       | Narration                                                                                                                                              | Visuals                                                                 | On-Screen Text                                 | Editing Notes                                        |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **0–3s**   | *"Have you ever wondered why space is completely dark, even though the Sun is incredibly bright?"*                                                     | Dramatic shot of the Sun in space, fading to a black background.        | **Why Is Space Black?**                        | Quick zoom, cinematic whoosh, hook immediately.      |

# Editing Notes

* Use fast cuts every **2–4 seconds** to maintain viewer attention.
* Keep on-screen text short and readable.
* Highlight key words with motion or color.
* Add captions for all narration.
* Avoid overcrowding the screen with text.
* Ensure visuals reinforce the spoken explanation.
