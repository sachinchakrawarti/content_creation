That gives your channel a much stronger identity than 50 unrelated videos.

### One recommendation

Don't make every episode simply **“Christianity explained.”** Make each episode answer a historical question.

For example:

* **Why did Christianity survive Roman persecution?**
* **Why did Paul become so important?**
* **Why did Constantine convert?**
* **Why did Christianity split into Catholic and Orthodox traditions?**
* **Why did Luther challenge the Catholic Church?**
* **How did Christianity become a global religion?**

That approach gives you **50 documentaries with 50 different hooks**, while still creating one enormous story.
