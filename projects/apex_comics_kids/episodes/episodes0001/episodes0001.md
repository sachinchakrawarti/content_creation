# Story

Samar is walking home through Apex City when he notices something strange inside his school locker.

His locker is glowing.

When he opens it, he discovers a tiny purple portal.

Samar calls Priyanka and Toto.

Toto scans the portal and suddenly says:

> "Warning! This portal is definitely not supposed to be in a school locker."

The portal suddenly grows.

A mysterious blue hand reaches through it.

Samar gets ready to investigate...

Then the hand pulls out a tiny banana.

Everyone freezes.

Toto says:

> "I have absolutely no explanation for this."

The portal disappears.

Samar looks at his friends.

> "Okay... tomorrow, we're checking that locker again."

The locker suddenly whispers:

> "Tomorrow..."

Samar's eyes widen.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
