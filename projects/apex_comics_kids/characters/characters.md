# Apex Comics Kids — Character Bible

**Project:** Apex Comics Kids
**Universe:** Apex City
**Language:** English
**Format:** Animated YouTube Shorts
**Primary Audience:** Kids and families
**Core Cast:** 20 heroes/companions
**Anti-Heroes:** 5

---

# 🌆 Apex City

Apex City is a colorful, modern fictional city where kids, heroes, teachers, animals, robots, and mysterious anti-heroes live together.

Everyday life can suddenly become an adventure. School days, friendships, competitions, strange discoveries, science experiments, and superhero mysteries all happen in Apex City.

The world should feel:

* Fun
* Colorful
* Safe
* Adventurous
* Funny
* Family-friendly
* Full of mystery

# 🎭 Character Consistency Rules

All Apex Comics Kids characters should remain visually and behaviorally consistent across episodes.

### Visual Consistency

* Same basic face
* Same hairstyle
* Same clothing design
* Same colors
* Same body proportions
* Same age appearance
* Same accessories
* Same character identity

### Voice Consistency

Each recurring character should have a dedicated voice identity.

**Do not randomly change a character's voice between episodes.**

Emotion can change:

> Happy → excited → scared → surprised → serious

But the underlying character voice should remain recognizable.

### Story Consistency

Characters should behave according to their established personalities.

A character can grow and change over time, but major changes should happen through the story rather than randomly between episodes.

# 🎬 Shorts Story Formula

Apex Comics Kids Shorts should generally follow:

**HOOK → PROBLEM → ADVENTURE → TWIST → FUN/EMOTIONAL ENDING**

Example:

**0–3 sec:** Something strange happens.
**3–15 sec:** Characters discover the problem.
**15–40 sec:** They try to solve it.
**40–50 sec:** Unexpected twist.
**50–60 sec:** Funny, exciting, or meaningful ending.

# 🌎 Global Audience

The series is designed for a worldwide English-speaking audience.

Stories should use:

* Simple English
* Clear dialogue
* Strong visual storytelling
* Universal humor
* Easy-to-understand emotions
* Fast pacing
* Family-friendly adventures

The goal is for a viewer to understand what is happening even if they are not familiar with the characters yet.

---

# ⭐ Core Universe Principle

**Apex City is the home of the characters.**

Every character should contribute something different:

**Heroes → Adventure**
**Friends → Comedy & Emotion**
**Animals → Fun & Action**
**Robot → Technology**
**Anti-Heroes → Mystery & Conflict**

The characters should feel like one connected universe rather than unrelated characters.
